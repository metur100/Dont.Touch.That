Privacy Policy

Don't Touch That! · Last updated: 9 October 2026

Short version: Don't Touch That! collects no personal data. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

Who is responsible

Don't Touch That! is developed and published by Medin Turkes, Düsseldorf, Germany. Contact: YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL@example.com.

What data the game processes

The game stores your progress on your own device only: scores, levels reached, streaks, statistics, achievements, daily challenge results and settings (sound, music, haptics, theme). This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or use Settings → Reset progress.

One type of level ("Don't shake the soda") reads the motion sensor (accelerometer) of your device while that level is running, only to detect whether the phone is being shaken. The readings are processed instantly on the device and are neither stored nor transmitted.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or logins
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services
  • No in-app purchases
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access
  • No internet connection is needed to play

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data at all, it also collects none from children.

Your rights

Since we do not receive or store any personal data, there is nothing we could give you access to, correct or delete. Your game data stays under your control on your device. If you have questions, contact us at the address above.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example by adding optional ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
